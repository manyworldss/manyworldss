# Hey, I'm Rapheal Suber

I build software around the people who use it, with a focus on human factors, accessibility, and thoughtful product design. My work brings together development, UX research, and a desire to make everyday experiences easier.

## Selected Work

### Prox

I worked as a contract UX engineer on Prox, an iOS grocery-savings app. It needs three to five minutes to gather local prices when someone signs up, and the old onboarding lost 65% of new users during that wait. I redesigned it into a 12-screen flow that explains what's happening and lets people leave while their deals load. Sign-ups rose 30–40%, and more than 500 users were onboarded.

Client work, so the code isn't public.

### Birk Aerosystems

I'm an engineering intern at an aerospace interiors manufacturer, where I build internal software and automation tools:

- **Internal audit tool:** organizes shipping, receiving, and quality records, flags inconsistencies, and sets up audits that a person still reviews. Built for 12 staff across two sites.
- **Supplier certification tracker:** one place for supplier certifications, with renewal tracking and automatic email reminders to suppliers.
- **Process automation:** I work with the people doing the manual work to find the slow spots and turn them into tools they can run themselves.

Internal tools, so the code isn't public.

### [Reach!](https://github.com/manyworldss/reach)

A caseload and outcome tracking platform for rehabilitation therapists supporting upper extremity recovery after stroke. Reach brings measured outcomes, recovery progress, and home program adherence into one clinician-facing interface. I built it during a UX for Healthcare certificate course on Maven, around feedback from three occupational therapists.

**Status:** Research prototype. It isn't for clinical use and won't be released, and every patient in the demo is synthetic.

[Live demo](https://reach-health.up.railway.app/) · [Repository](https://github.com/manyworldss/reach)

### [Celio](https://github.com/manyworldss/Celio)

An iPhone app that helps people with celiac disease communicate their dietary needs across languages and hear back from the person preparing their food. It works offline and requires no account.

**Status:** Closed beta with 50+ testers in 3+ countries. It isn't going to the App Store, because an app about a medical condition should have clinical review behind it. The public repository holds Celio's marketing website.

[Website](https://manyworldss.github.io/Celio/) · [Repository](https://github.com/manyworldss/Celio)

### [Project North Star](https://github.com/manyworldss/Project-North-Star)

A platform for measuring automation bias: how often people change a correct answer after an AI confidently gives them a wrong one. It looks at the person's decision, not the model's accuracy.

[Live demo](https://north-star-prototype.up.railway.app) · [Repository](https://github.com/manyworldss/Project-North-Star)
